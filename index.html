The company a word keeps, drawn from real text on the web.

Pick a word

Contexter collects hundreds of real sentences containing your word, looks at the words around it, and draws them as a network.

  • Circle area = how often a neighbor appears near the word
  • Short, thick line = the two words often appear together
  • Long, faint line = they rarely do
